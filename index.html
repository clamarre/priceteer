The idea

Less searching. Better context.

Priceteer is designed to surface comparable offers while you shop, with enough detail to understand what makes one price different from another.

Recognize the product

Use model numbers, UPCs, and product details to avoid comparing similar-looking but different items.

How future retailer relationships may work.

Priceteer may participate in affiliate programs. If a shopper follows an eligible link and completes a purchase, Priceteer may earn a commission at no additional cost to the shopper.

Affiliate compensation will not guarantee placement or change the product-matching standards used by the extension. Participating relationships will be labeled clearly.
